Privacy Policy

First Bites · Effective October 30, 2026

First Bites is a family food and allergen tracking application for adult parents and caregivers. It allows caregivers to create shared households, add child profiles, and record foods, favorites, feeding notes, and possible reactions. This policy explains what information First Bites collects, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

First Bites is not directed to children. Adult caregivers may enter information about children in their care. The app is a tracking tool and does not provide medical diagnosis or individualized medical advice.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use First Bites, we may collect or process:

  • Account information: your name or display name, email address, account identifier, authentication information handled by our authentication provider, and—when you choose Google sign-in—your Google profile picture URL.
  • Household information: household name, household membership, role, and invite code.
  • Child information: a child’s name and optional birth date.
  • Feeding and health-related information: foods logged, dates and times, amount or description, likes/dislikes, favorites, allergen exposures, possible reaction status, first-exposure status, and notes you choose to enter.
  • Technical information: our hosting and database providers may process IP address, approximate city/country derived from IP address, browser/device information, request logs, and diagnostic or security information needed to operate and protect the service.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate users; create and manage households and child profiles; synchronize food history between authorized household members; display activity and progress; maintain favorites and allergen tracking; secure the service; troubleshoot problems; and operate First Bites.

Shared household data

Information associated with a household or child is visible to other signed-in users who have been added to that household. A household invite code is intended to be shared only with caregivers you want to give access. First Bites does not make household or child information public.

Service providers

First Bites uses service providers to operate the application:

  • Supabase provides authentication, database hosting, and synchronization.
  • Vercel hosts and delivers the First Bites web application.
  • Google is used only when you choose “Continue with Google” for authentication.

These providers may process information on our behalf in order to provide, secure, and maintain their services. First Bites does not sell personal information and does not use personal information for advertising.

Data security

First Bites uses HTTPS for data transmitted between your device and its service providers. Database access is restricted to authenticated users and uses household-based access controls so users can access only households to which they belong. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data retention

Account and application data are retained while needed to provide First Bites and until they are deleted or an account-deletion request is completed. If you leave or are removed from a shared household, household information may remain available to the household’s remaining members. When an account is deleted, personal account information and data associated specifically with that account will be deleted or de-identified as appropriate; shared household information may remain where it belongs to other household members.

Account and data deletion

You may permanently delete your First Bites account and associated personal data at any time from Settings → Privacy & account → Delete my account. Signed-in self-service deletion is processed immediately after confirmation. If you cannot sign in, you may use the public deletion page below to request deletion after account ownership is verified.

Account and data deletion

Your choices

You can edit profile, household, and child information within the app. Household owners can remove other household members, and non-owner members can leave a household. You can also stop using First Bites and request account deletion.

Children’s privacy

First Bites is intended for adult parents and caregivers and is not designed for use by children. Adults are responsible for information they choose to enter about children in their care.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as First Bites changes. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date shown above and publish the revised policy at this URL.
