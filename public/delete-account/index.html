FBFirst Bites Privacy Policy

Delete your First Bites account

This page is available even if you no longer have First Bites installed.

You can request permanent deletion of your First Bites account and associated personal data. To protect your account, please send the request from the email address you use to sign in to First Bites.

How to request deletion

  1. Tap the button below to create an email to the First Bites privacy contact.
  2. Send the message from the email address associated with your First Bites account.
  3. We may reply if additional verification is necessary.
Request account deletion Email manually

What will be deleted

Your authentication account, First Bites profile, household memberships, and personal data associated with your account will be deleted. Food logs, notes, and favorites specifically associated with your account will also be removed or de-identified as appropriate.

Shared household information

If you belong to a household with other caregivers, information that belongs to the shared household or child may remain for the other household members after your personal account information and attribution are removed. If you are the only member of a household, that household and its child data can be deleted with your account.

If you are a household owner: deletion may require transferring household ownership to another caregiver or deleting the household if no other members remain.

Retention

Deletion requests are processed after account ownership is verified. Information may be retained only when reasonably necessary for security, legal, or fraud-prevention obligations; otherwise the requested account data will be deleted.
