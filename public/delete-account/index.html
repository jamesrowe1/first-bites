FBFirst Bites Privacy Policy

Delete your First Bites account

This page is available even if you no longer have First Bites installed.

If you can still sign in to First Bites, you can permanently delete your account yourself from Settings → Privacy & account → Delete my account. The deletion happens immediately after you confirm it.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to First Bites.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Under Privacy & account, tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE when prompted to confirm permanent deletion.
Open First Bites

If you cannot sign in

You can still request deletion from outside the app. Send the request from the email address associated with your First Bites account so account ownership can be verified.

Request deletion by email

What will be deleted

Your authentication account, First Bites profile, household memberships, and personal data associated with your account will be deleted. Food logs, notes, and favorites specifically associated with your account will also be removed or de-identified as appropriate.

Shared household information

If you belong to a household with other caregivers, information that belongs to the shared household or child may remain for the other household members after your personal account information and attribution are removed. If you are the only member of a household, that household and its child data can be deleted with your account.

If you are a household owner: deletion may require transferring household ownership to another caregiver or deleting the household if no other members remain.

Retention

Self-service deletion in the app is processed immediately after signed-in confirmation. Requests submitted by email are processed after account ownership is verified. Information may be retained only when reasonably necessary for security, legal, or fraud-prevention obligations; otherwise the requested account data will be deleted.
